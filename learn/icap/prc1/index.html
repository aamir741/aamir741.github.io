Frequently asked questions

Is there a free PRC-1 study guide?

Yes. The PRC-1 Fundamentals of Accounting Quick Revision Notes by Laiba Jamil, CA, are a free 58-page PDF with the key rules, must-know formats, worked examples, exam traps and quick self-tests for all 10 chapters. Request them on this page: continue with Google, and we email you a download link.

Is PRC-1 Exam Prep really free?

Yes. Sign in with Google and start straight away with 100 MCQs and 1 full mock paper. Then send a free, one-click access request to unlock all 1,000 MCQs, more mock papers and your readiness score.

What is the mock exam pattern?

Each mock has 40 MCQs (2 marks each) and one structured financial-statement question (20 marks) where you enter the figures yourself, 120 minutes, 100 marks, marked as soon as you submit. The pass mark is 50%.

Are these ICAP's own questions?

No. Every question is written for this site, following the ICAP PRC-1 syllabus (Education and Training Scheme 2025). Use it alongside ICAP's study text and question bank.

Are there 'select all that apply' questions?

Yes. Some MCQs have more than one correct answer. You must pick exactly the right options to get the marks, as in a computer-based exam.

Which chapters are covered?

The ten chapters of the PRC-1 study text: accounting basics, books of prime entry, ledgers and trial balance, accruals and prepayments, bad and doubtful debts, property plant and equipment, inventories, correction of errors, bank reconciliations and preparation of financial statements. All ten are live, with 100 MCQs each.
