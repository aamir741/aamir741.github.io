PRC-1 · Chapter 4

Chapter 4 MCQs: Accruals and prepayments

This chapter explains why financial statements are prepared on the accrual basis rather than the cash basis. It covers accrued and prepaid expenses, accrued and unearned income, how to time-apportion amounts to the right period, and how to record them using the two-account system or a single memorandum account. It also shows how missing adjustments change profit and the statement of financial position.

What Chapter 4 covers

  • Accrual and cash basis
  • Accrued expenses
  • Prepaid expenses
  • Accrued income
  • Unearned income
  • Time apportionment
  • Single-account (memorandum) method
  • Two-account journal entries
  • Effect on profit and financial position
  • Correcting omitted adjustments
