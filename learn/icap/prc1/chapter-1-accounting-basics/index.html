PRC-1 · Chapter 1

Chapter 1 MCQs: Accounting basics

This chapter explains how book-keeping and accounting record and report a business's transactions, who uses financial statements and the concepts that guide them. It also covers the elements of financial statements, capital versus revenue items, the accounting equation and the rules of debit and credit.

What Chapter 1 covers

  • Book-keeping and accounting
  • Business transactions
  • Capital and revenue items
  • Elements of financial statements
  • Accounting equation
  • Accounting concepts
  • Qualitative characteristics
  • Users of financial statements
  • Rules of debit and credit
