CAF-3 DSR · Chapter 2

Chapter 2 MCQs: Data Governance & Management

Chapter 2 covers how organisations govern and protect their data: the components and levels of data governance, data classification, storage options, data integrity, data security, stewardship, metadata, compliance and the emerging technologies that support governance.

What Chapter 2 covers

  • What data governance is and why it matters
  • The seven components and three levels (strategic, tactical, operational)
  • Data classification models and levels
  • Data storage: on-premise, cloud, hybrid, data lakes and edge
  • Data integrity types and practices
  • Data security practices and emerging threats
  • Stewardship, metadata, compliance and privacy-enhancing technologies

Need to revise first? Chapter 2 is explained in plain language, with exam tips and worked scenarios, in the free CAF-3 DSR study guide (PDF). More chapters: CAF-3 DSR practice hub.
