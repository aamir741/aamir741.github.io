Chapter 2 MCQs: Data Governance & Management

Chapter 2 covers how organisations govern and protect their data: the components and levels of data governance, data classification, storage options, data integrity, data security, stewardship, metadata, compliance and the emerging technologies that support governance.

Sample questions from Chapter 2

1. The board of Indus Telecom Ltd appoints a senior executive who is accountable for the customer data domain and decides who may access it and how it may be used, but who does not personally carry out daily data cleansing. This role is BEST described as:

  1. Data owner
  2. Data steward
  3. Data custodian
  4. Data governance council
Show answer

A. Data owner Data Owners are senior leaders holding accountability and strategic decision rights over a data domain; day-to-day maintenance is delegated to Data Stewards.

2. An e-commerce marketplace in Karachi moves order records from 2015, rarely accessed but legally required to be kept, onto cheaper, slower storage rather than deleting them. This is which stage of the data lifecycle?

  1. Usage
  2. Archiving
  3. Creation
  4. Deletion / disposal
Show answer

B. Archiving Moving inactive-but-retained data to lower-cost, long-term storage is Archiving, distinct from permanent removal (Deletion).

3. A shipment entered as 03/04/2025 (3 April) in the warehouse system is read as 4 March by the export system, because the two systems order the day and month differently. This is BEST described as:

  1. A data format issue
  2. Duplicate data
  3. Missing data
  4. Data latency
Show answer

A. A data format issue Dates held as DD/MM/YYYY in one system and MM/DD/YYYY in another is the guide's example of a data format issue. Nothing is missing or repeated here.
