Frequently asked questions

Are these CAF-3 DSR MCQs free?

Yes. All chapter practice is free. You fill in a short form once (name, email, mobile, city and institute) and then practise as often as you like.

Do the questions follow the ICAP exam style?

Yes. Questions are written to the ICAP CAF-3 Data, Systems and Risks syllabus, including the scenario-based MCQs that appear in Section A of the paper, and every question has an explanation.

Can I choose how many questions to practise?

Yes. Pick 10, 20, 30, all questions in a chapter, or type any number. Learn mode shows the answer after each question; Test mode is timed and shows results at the end.

Is there a full mock exam?

Yes. The mock exam builder creates a paper from any chapters you choose in the ICAP pattern: Question 1 (20 MCQs) online and timed, and Questions 2 to 7 as a downloadable PDF with separate model answers. Questions are drawn at random, so every student gets a different paper.
