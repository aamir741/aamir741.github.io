Frequently asked questions

Is CAF-3 DSR Exam Prep free?

You can start free. Sign in with your Google account, add your mobile, city and institute, and practise up to 100 MCQs from any chapters plus 1 full mock paper. To keep going, request full access with one click on any practice page.

What does full access include?

Full access unlocks every MCQ in all 17 chapters, more mock papers in the ICAP pattern, AI marking of your handwritten answers and your exam readiness score. Request it from any practice page; we review each request and email you as soon as it is approved.

Why do I need to sign in with Google?

Your Google account keeps your free allowance, your results and your access together on your phone and laptop. We only receive your name and email address from Google, never your password.

Do the questions follow the ICAP exam style?

Yes. Questions are written to the ICAP CAF-3 Data, Systems and Risks syllabus, including the scenario-based MCQs that appear in Section A of the paper, and every question has an explanation.

Can I choose how many questions to practise?

Yes. Pick 10, 20, 30, all questions in a chapter, or type any number. Learn mode shows the answer after each question; Test mode is timed and shows results at the end.

Is there a full mock exam?

Yes. The mock exam builder creates a paper from any chapters you choose in the ICAP pattern: Question 1 (15 MCQs, 15 marks) online and timed, and Questions 2 to 7 (85 marks) as a downloadable PDF with separate model answers. If you only want MCQs, choose an MCQ-only mock and pick how many questions you want (up to 100). Questions are drawn at random, so every student gets a different paper. Your first mock paper is free; more papers come with full access.

How does AI marking of my written answers work?

AI marking is part of full access. After Question 1, write your answers to Questions 2 to 7 on paper, then take a clear photo of each page (or scan them into one PDF) and upload them. The AI reads your handwriting, marks every part against the model answer and marking guidance, and gives you marks, feedback and your total out of 100 within a minute or two. You can have up to 2 papers marked per day. Write only your paper code on the sheets, not your name.

Is the AI mark accurate?

It is a good estimate, not an official result. Like a fair examiner, it gives credit for valid points worded differently from the model answer, but clear handwriting and well-lit photos give the fairest marks. Check any mark you disagree with against the model-answer PDF.

What is the readiness score?

Part of full access: your exam readiness score (out of 100) on the My progress page combines your recent MCQ accuracy and your marked written answers for every chapter, weighted by the ICAP syllabus (Data 35-45%, Systems 30-40%, Risks 20-30%). A chapter counts fully once you have answered about 20 questions in it, and the page lists the chapters you should work on next.

Is my data safe?

Your details and results are kept in a private sheet that only mirakx.com can access, and are never sold. Uploaded answer sheets are marked by Google's Gemini AI and stored privately. Read the full privacy notice at mirakx.com/caf3/privacy/.
