Chapter 7 MCQs: Information Systems Architecture

Chapter 7 explains information systems architecture: the key components, how they interact, scalability and flexibility, design best practices, emerging trends and an introduction to programming languages.

What Chapter 7 covers

  • The four key components of IT architecture
  • Interactions and dependencies, networks and storage
  • Horizontal and vertical scaling, flexibility
  • Best practices for designing IT architecture
  • Emerging trends such as virtualisation and cloud
  • Introduction to programming languages
