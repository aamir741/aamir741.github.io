CAF-3 DSR · Chapter 1

Chapter 1 MCQs: Types of Data & Sources

Chapter 1 builds the foundation for the whole CAF-3 syllabus: what data is, how it differs from information, the four types of data, the three structural categories, where data comes from, what makes data collection good, and the ethical and legal rules around personal data.

What Chapter 1 covers

  • Data vs information
  • Qualitative (nominal, ordinal) and quantitative (discrete, continuous) data
  • Structured, unstructured and semi-structured data
  • Internal vs external and primary vs secondary sources, including APIs
  • Relevance, accuracy and timeliness of data collection
  • Privacy, transparency, security and fairness
  • PECA 2016, ETO 2002, National Cyber Security Policy, GDPR and CCPA

Need to revise first? Chapter 1 is explained in plain language, with exam tips and worked scenarios, in the free CAF-3 DSR study guide (PDF). More chapters: CAF-3 DSR practice hub.
