Chapter 17 MCQs: ICT's Role in Risk Management

Chapter 17 explains ICT's role in risk management: modern risk categories, and how ICT supports risk identification, reporting, management and mitigation, with best practices for integrating ICT into risk management.

Sample questions from Chapter 17

1. A diversified conglomerate's board notes that ICT itself introduces new risks (e.g., cyberattacks) while simultaneously providing the tools needed to identify, report, and mitigate those same risks. This reflects which characteristic of ICT's role in modern risk management?

  1. A dual role: it creates new risks and provides tools to manage them
  2. A purely risk-creating role, offering no real benefit in mitigation
  3. A minor role, since risk management is mainly a manual activity
  4. A risk-eliminating role, since its tools remove all risk entirely
Show answer

A. A dual role: it creates new risks and provides tools to manage them ICT plays a dual role: it introduces new risks such as cyberattacks while also providing the tools to identify, report and mitigate risk, including the risks it creates.

2. A regional bank wants earlier detection of fraud, cyberattacks, and operational anomalies, and is considering implementing AI-based analytics, SIEM, and vulnerability scanning together. Which combination of ICT capabilities does this reflect?

  1. Risk identification tools
  2. Risk reporting tools
  3. Regulatory compliance tools
  4. Business continuity tools
Show answer

A. Risk identification tools AI-based analytics, SIEM real-time monitoring and vulnerability scanning are all risk identification tools aimed at earlier detection. Reporting tools communicate risks once they are identified.

3. A listed modaraba's system automatically compiles its SECP returns from the accounting ledger, tracks filing deadlines and keeps a clear audit trail. Which risk reporting tool is this?

  1. Dashboards and visualisation
  2. Regulatory compliance tools
  3. Centralised risk repository
  4. Automated risk control system
Show answer

B. Regulatory compliance tools Automating compliance reporting to meet regulatory deadlines with a clear audit trail is the role of regulatory compliance tools. An automated risk control system is a mitigation tool such as a self-healing network.
