Chapter 5 MCQs: Database Management Systems

Chapter 5 covers Database Management Systems: what a DBMS is, its five components, the ACID properties, data independence, the three-level schema architecture and the four database models.

What Chapter 5 covers

  • What a DBMS is and why it is used
  • The five components of a DBMS
  • ACID: atomicity, consistency, isolation, durability
  • Additional DBMS characteristics
  • Logical and physical data independence
  • The three-level schema architecture
  • Hierarchical, network, relational and NoSQL models
