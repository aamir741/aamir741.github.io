Privacy notice

CAF-3 DSR Exam Prep · last updated 28 October 2026

What we collect

  • Your details: you sign in with Continue with Google, and we receive your name and verified email address from Google; we do not receive your password or access anything else in your Google account. You then add your mobile number, city and institute.
  • Your practice results: chapter, mode, number of questions, score and time taken, for every practice or mock you finish, and your score by chapter, used to calculate your readiness score.
  • Your access: which questions and mock papers you have been given (so you see new questions first and the free allowance can be counted), and whether you have asked for or been given full access, with any note you add to your request.
  • Answer sheets you upload for AI marking: the photos or PDF of your handwritten Questions 2 to 7, and the marks and feedback given.
  • Technical details: the page used and your browser type.

AI marking

Uploaded answer sheets are sent to Google's Gemini AI service to be marked against the model answers. Gemini is used on Google's free tier, under which Google may use submitted content to improve its products. Please write only your paper code on your answer sheets, not your name or other personal details. AI marks are an estimate, not an official result. Uploaded sheets are kept in a private Google Drive folder that only mirakx.com can access, so that marks can be checked.

Your best scores and recent mock papers are also kept in your own browser.

Why

  • To understand how students are performing by chapter and improve the questions.
  • To contact you, by email, phone or WhatsApp, about CAF-3 DSR study material, classes and updates. You can ask us to stop at any time.

Where it is stored and who sees it

Your details and results are stored in a private Google Sheet that only mirakx.com can access. With full access, the “My progress” page shows you your own results from any device; nobody else can see them there. They are not sold or shared with anyone else. This site also uses Google Analytics, which does not receive your name, email or phone number.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete your data, or to stop being contacted, email aamir@mirakx.com. You can change your mobile, city and institute at any time with “Change details” on any practice page.
