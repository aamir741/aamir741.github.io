Free CISA Complete Study Guide: 352 Pages, All Five Domains, 100 Practice Questions

Download the free CISA Complete Study Guide: all five CISA domains in plain language, 100 exam-style questions with explained answers, memory aids and glossaries.

Muhammad Aamir · 10 November 2026

The CISA Complete Study Guide is finished, and you can download it free. It is a 352-page PDF for anyone preparing for the Certified Information Systems Auditor exam.

👉 Download the free PDF

What's inside

  • All five CISA domains, from the audit process and IT governance through acquisition, operations and resilience, and protection of information assets
  • 100 exam-style practice questions, each with its answer explained
  • The Last Mile sections: memory aids and exam traps at the end of each domain
  • A glossary for every domain, so unfamiliar terms are explained where you need them

Who it's for

Some IT audit experience helps, but it isn't required. Terms are explained in the text, and the book is written so you can understand an idea and say it in your own words, because a memorised phrase is fragile when the exam changes the scenario.

How to use it

Read one section, then watch the matching lesson in the free CISA video course on the Mirak X YouTube channel. Finish each domain with its practice questions, and go back to anything you got wrong. For more timed practice, try CertPrepX.

Find the book, the lessons and the study notes in one place at mirakx.com/learn/cisa.
