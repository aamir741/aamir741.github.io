500,000 Chromebooks for Students: How to Apply for the PM Youth Chromebook Scheme

Who is eligible for Pakistan's PM Youth Chromebook Scheme, how to apply on the IBCC portal, the 4 documents you need, and how to avoid registration scams.

Muhammad Aamir · 26 October 2026

Pakistan has launched the Prime Minister's Youth Chromebook Scheme, with plans to distribute 500,000 Chromebooks over two years to support digital learning. Reports say students will receive refurbished Chromebooks along with an internet device, to help with online classes, research, e-libraries and other educational resources. (PhoneWorld)

🎓 Who can apply?

According to the eligibility criteria on the official IBCC portal:

Region Who is eligible
Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan Top-performing Grade 9 students from government/public schools
Islamabad, AJK, Gilgit-Baltistan Top-performing Grade 9 and Grade 10 students from government/public schools

Eligibility is based on the Annual Examination 2026 results. Being eligible does not guarantee a Chromebook, because selection depends on performance and available quotas.

📝 How to apply

  1. Visit the official portal: pmychromebooks.ibcc.edu.pk
  2. Select your education board.
  3. Enter your CNIC/B-Form, roll number or registration number (numbers only, no dashes), and your date of birth.
  4. Click "Find My Record" and check your details (name, school and marks come from your board's list).
  5. Upload the required documents, review your application carefully and submit.
  6. Download and keep your application slip and reference number.

📂 Documents to prepare

  • A recent colour photograph (front-facing, plain background)
  • Your SSC-I or SSC-II mark sheet
  • An attested copy of your B-Form/CNIC
  • An attested copy of your father's or guardian's CNIC

Each file must be 300 KB or smaller. Photographs must be JPG or PNG; the other documents can also be PDF.

⚠️ Important

  • Registration is free. No fee appears on the portal or in board notices. Do not pay anyone to register a student, and only use the official link above: fake links and "agents" are a common scam. (Eduvision)
  • Deadlines differ by board. Some boards' deadlines may already have passed, so check your own board's announcement.
  • Sindh students: registration opens after all Sindh boards announce their Class 9 Annual Examination 2026 results.
  • One application per student. You cannot edit the form yourself after submission. If your record is missing, try another identification option, then contact your education board.

Check the official portal and your board's announcements for the latest updates.
