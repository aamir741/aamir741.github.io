Mirak X NextGen: An Exam-Prep Platform That Marks Handwritten Answers with AI

Mirak X NextGen gives institutes practice, mock exams and AI marking of handwritten answers. Live for ICAP CAF-3 DSR, ready for other subjects.

Muhammad Aamir · 30 October 2026

Most online exam practice stops at MCQs. But in professional exams like ICAP's, a large share of the marks come from written answers, and students don't get those answers marked until it is too late: a teacher with 200 scripts can return them only so fast.

Mirak X NextGen closes that gap. Students write their answers by hand, on paper, the way they will in the exam, upload photos, and get marks and feedback for every part within minutes. It is live today for ICAP CAF-3 Data, Systems and Risks at mirakx.com/caf3, and it is built to run any subject.

How AI marking works

  1. Unique paper. The platform builds a fresh paper for each student in the exam's own pattern and gives it a paper code.
  2. Write by hand. The student answers on paper, under time, writing only the paper code on the sheets, never their name.
  3. Upload sheets. Phone photos or one scanned PDF, uploaded from the mock page.
  4. AI reads and marks. A vision AI model reads the handwriting and marks each part against the model answer and marking guide.
  5. Get feedback. Marks per part, the points that earned them, the points that were missing, and a total out of 100 together with the online MCQ section.

The AI marks like a fair examiner: it credits valid points written in the student's own words, gives partial credit, and never awards more than a part is worth. Every answer sheet is archived and every result recorded, so teachers can review any mark. AI marks are a strong estimate, not an official result.

For CAF-3 alone that means 1,020 MCQs, 136 written and scenario questions and all 17 chapters, each checked against the ICAP syllabus and study text.

Build it for your institute

The engine is not tied to CAF-3. Any subject with a syllabus and a known paper pattern can run on it, including subjects with long written answers: other ICAP CAF and CFAP papers, ACCA, CIMA, CISA, university exams, and O and A Level theory papers.

Ready now

  • New subjects on the same platform, configured to your paper pattern and syllabus weightings
  • Question bank development: original questions written to the syllabus, each with an explanation or a model answer and marking guide, checked for syllabus relevance and common flaws
  • Import your own questions: your existing MCQs and mock questions, cleaned up and given marking guides so the AI can mark against them

On request

  • Institute branding: your name, colours and domain
  • Teacher dashboard: results by batch and student, weak chapters across the class, and a screen to confirm or adjust AI marks
  • Batch enrolment: automatic access for your students by class list or email domain
  • Scheduled class tests: one paper for a whole batch at a set time, with scripts marked overnight

Start with a pilot

  1. Choose a subject, its chapters and paper pattern.
  2. Build and review the bank: we prepare the questions, model answers and marking guides; your teachers review them.
  3. Run with one batch: students practise and sit mocks; teachers compare AI marks with their own on a sample of scripts.
  4. Review and scale together, then roll out to more batches.
