Mirak X NextGen: An Exam-Prep Platform That Marks Handwritten Answers with AI

Mirak X NextGen is a free exam-prep project with practice, mock exams and AI marking of handwritten answers, live for ICAP CAF-3 DSR students.

Muhammad Aamir · 30 October 2026

Most online exam practice stops at MCQs. But in professional exams like ICAP's, a large share of the marks come from written answers, and students don't get those answers marked until it is too late: a teacher with 200 scripts can return them only so fast.

Mirak X NextGen closes that gap. Students write their answers by hand, on paper, the way they will in the exam, upload photos, and get marks and feedback for every part within minutes. It is live today for ICAP CAF-3 Data, Systems and Risks at mirakx.com/learn/icap/caf3, and it is built so it can run other subjects too.

How AI marking works

  1. Unique paper. The platform builds a fresh paper for each student in the exam's own pattern and gives it a paper code.
  2. Write by hand. The student answers on paper, under time, writing only the paper code on the sheets, never their name.
  3. Upload sheets. Phone photos or one scanned PDF, uploaded from the mock page.
  4. AI reads and marks. A vision AI model reads the handwriting and marks each part against the model answer and marking guide.
  5. Get feedback. Marks per part, the points that earned them, the points that were missing, and a total out of 100 together with the online MCQ section.

The AI marks like a fair examiner: it credits valid points written in the student's own words, gives partial credit, and never awards more than a part is worth. Every answer sheet is archived and every result recorded, so any mark can be reviewed. AI marks are a strong estimate, not an official result.

One connected platform

Area What it does
Chapter-wise practice Syllabus-checked MCQs with explanations, Learn mode and timed Test mode, unseen questions first, retry wrong answers
Exam-style mocks A different paper for every student, from any combination of chapters, in the real paper pattern
Readiness analytics A readiness score out of 100, per-chapter mastery weighted by the syllabus, and what to work on next
Secure student access Google sign-in, approval-based access, daily usage limits, question bank kept on the server
Simple administration Access approvals, attempt records, marking records and a daily summary email
Low-maintenance setup Works on any phone or laptop, serverless, very low running cost

For CAF-3 alone that means 1,020 MCQs, 136 written and scenario questions and all 17 chapters, each checked against the ICAP syllabus and study text.
